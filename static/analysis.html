Method notes and interpretation limits

Total energy (Eh): Electronic energy of the complete model; it is not an affinity score across different oligomer sizes.

E/atom and Δ group: Rough diagnostics only. Adsorption requires matched complex-minus-fragment interaction energies.

HOMO–LUMO gap: Electronic descriptor. Gradient: residual force-like convergence metric; smaller is better.

ALPB water: Implicit aqueous environment; this is not explicit-water MD or a free-energy calculation.
